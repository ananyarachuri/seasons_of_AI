Reading the climate

Two schools

SymbolicvsNeural

Symbolic AI wrote intelligence by hand as rules. It was clear but brittle, and it froze both winters.

Connectionism learns from data. Written off twice, it won once GPUs and big datasets arrived in 2012.

Narrow ≠ General

Narrow AI masters one task. Deep Blue plays chess; AlphaFold folds proteins. Everything on this page is narrow, including today's chatbots.

AGI would match people at almost any intellectual task. It is still a goal and an argument, not a product.
